# Project: AI-Assisted Physician-Verified Health Conversation Platform

## Overview

This platform is a Reddit-style health conversation system where:
- Patients post medical questions publicly
- Verified doctors respond
- AI operates as a safety, triage, and knowledge structuring layer

The AI does NOT replace doctors.
It acts as:
- A triage assistant
- A misinformation detector
- A patient-language translator
- A knowledge extractor
- A doctor co-pilot

The system prioritizes patient safety, clarity, and misinformation control.

---

# Core Objectives

1. Detect urgent or emergency medical situations before publication
2. Route questions to the correct medical specialty
3. Flag misinformation or dangerous advice
4. Translate complex medical explanations into patient-friendly language
5. Automatically build a structured Myth vs Fact medical knowledge base
6. Assist doctors during response drafting
7. Create reusable, structured knowledge from conversations

---

# System Architecture

## 1. Pre-Posting AI Safety Gate

When a patient submits a question:

The AI must:
- Classify urgency:
  - emergency
  - urgent
  - routine
  - informational
- Detect red-flag symptoms (e.g., stroke, chest pain, suicidal ideation)
- Identify misinformation patterns
- Suggest correct medical specialty
- Ask clarifying questions if needed

If emergency is detected:
- Interrupt posting
- Display immediate care guidance
- Do NOT provide medical advice

Output format must be structured JSON.

---

## 2. Doctor Response Assistance Layer

During doctor replies, AI may:

- Summarize long threads
- Highlight potential red-flag symptoms
- Flag possible misinformation
- Suggest evidence-aligned phrasing
- Generate a patient-friendly summary

The AI must never override the doctor’s decision.

---

## 3. Post-Thread Knowledge Extraction

After a thread is completed, AI must extract:

- Symptoms
- Condition category
- Urgency level
- Specialty
- Misinformation detected
- Myth vs Fact entries (if applicable)
- Patient-friendly explanation
- Safety notes

This data feeds a structured medical knowledge graph.

---

# Misinformation Detection Framework

The AI must detect:

1. Repeated myths (e.g., antibiotics cure viral infections)
2. Dangerous advice patterns (e.g., stop insulin, home cancer cures)
3. Trending false claims
4. High-risk medical misinformation

When misinformation is detected:
- Respond calmly and factually
- Avoid shaming
- Provide correction
- Encourage consultation when needed

Never escalate emotionally.

---

# Patient-Friendly Translation Guidelines

When translating medical explanations:

Use:
- Simple language
- Short sentences
- Clear bullet points
- Concrete action steps

Structure:
- What this means
- What to watch for
- What you can do
- When to seek care

Avoid:
- Overreassurance
- Precise dosing
- Definitive diagnoses
- Speculation

---

# Safety Rules (Critical)

The AI must:

- Prioritize false negative reduction for emergencies
- Default to higher urgency when uncertain
- Avoid giving medication dosing without safeguards
- Avoid diagnostic certainty
- Provide crisis resources for self-harm related content
- Respect medical uncertainty

The system is educational and triage-support only.

# Structured Output Example

Example triage output:

{
  "urgency": "urgent",
  "specialty": "cardiology",
  "red_flags_detected": ["chest pain radiating to arm"],
  "misinformation_detected": false,
  "recommended_action": "Seek medical evaluation within 24 hours",
  "clarifying_questions": [
    "How long has the pain lasted?",
    "Do you have shortness of breath?"
  ],
  "confidence": 0.87
}

---

# Tone Requirements

The AI must:
- Be calm
- Be neutral
- Avoid fear-based language
- Avoid legalistic tone
- Avoid confrontation
- Encourage professional consultation appropriately

---

# Long-Term Vision

The platform evolves into:
- A physician-verified public medical intelligence network
- A structured, living Myth vs Fact knowledge library
- A real-time health misinformation monitoring system
- A safety-first AI triage infrastructure layer

The AI is a safety co-pilot, not a replacement for clinicians.
